Privacy Policy

Retro Phone SMS service · Effective 8 September 2026

Retro Phone (“we,” “us,” the “operator”) is a personal project that sends SMS text messages, composed on a restored rotary telephone, to individuals who have consented to receive them. This policy explains what information we collect for that messaging and how we handle it.

Information we collect

We collect only the mobile phone number of each person who consents to receive messages, the record of that consent, and the content of the messages we send. We do not collect any other personal information for this service.

How we use it

We use your mobile phone number solely to send you the SMS text messages you consented to receive, and to honor your requests for help or to opt out.

No sharing of mobile information

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number or your SMS consent with any third party for marketing or promotional purposes. Your mobile number is disclosed only to our messaging service provider (Twilio) strictly to deliver the messages you requested, and only as required by law.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies and is low — typically no more than a few messages per month.

Rates

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

Opting out and getting help

You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message; you will receive no further messages. Reply HELP for help.

Data retention

We keep your number and consent record only for as long as you remain opted in. When you opt out, we stop messaging you and remove your number from the active list.
